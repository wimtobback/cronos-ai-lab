## The problem

Routine imaging was taking up to four days to report, not because radiologists were slow but because the worklist was effectively first-in-first-out across five sites with very different staffing and specialisms.

## What we did

No diagnostic AI. We were clear from the framing sprint that we were not going to read scans — we were going to fix the queue.

The system matches each study to available radiologists by subspecialty, urgency, prior-study continuity and current load, and rebalances across sites continuously. Clinical urgency always outranks efficiency; a radiologist can always override.

## Outcome

94% of routine studies reported same day, up from 38%. No change in headcount. The most common piece of feedback was that radiologists saw more of the cases they were actually best at.
