## The problem

The association's tenant helpline was overwhelmed by questions with knowable answers: rent status, repair progress, waiting-list position. Staff spent their day looking things up in three systems while vulnerable tenants waited.

## What we did

We rejected the association's initial brief, which was for a chatbot. The bottleneck was not conversation — it was that no single system knew a tenant's full situation.

We built a unified tenant view first, then a self-service layer over it in Dutch and French, at a reading level tested with actual tenants. Anything involving money owed, eviction risk or vulnerability flags routes straight to a person, by design.

## Outcome

71% of contacts resolved without staff involvement. Queue time at peak fell from 25 minutes to under 4. Staff time moved to the cases that needed judgement.
