## The problem

A small share of claims escalate into complaints, ombudsman cases or litigation, and they consume a wildly disproportionate share of cost and goodwill. Handlers could often tell which ones were heading that way, but only once they were already there.

## What we did

We modelled escalation risk from the claim record and the correspondence around it, then — the part that actually mattered — designed what happens when the score is high. A risk score with no intervention attached changes nothing.

High-risk claims are reassigned to senior handlers with a suggested next action within one working day.

## Outcome

31% fewer escalations year on year in the covered lines. The model was the easy half; the operating change was the reason it worked.
