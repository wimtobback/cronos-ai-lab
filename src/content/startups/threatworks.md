Security budgets are finite. Attack surfaces are not. ThreatWorks is a specialist security consultancy working across the Netherlands and Belgium, partnering with engineering, product and leadership teams to make security a strategic advantage rather than a constraint.

## Secure by design, not secured afterwards

The practice runs as a loop. Map assets, trust boundaries and abuse cases before a line of code is written. Translate those threats into concrete controls and acceptance criteria. Verify every requirement through scanners and automated penetration tests. Retest the fixes against the original attack paths, and start again.

## What they do

Cybersecurity architecture and threat modelling. Risk prioritisation ranked by business impact and exploitability. Manual application and infrastructure penetration testing. Red-team simulations that exercise technology, detection and response together. Engagements run from a single focused operation to a continuous partnership with quarterly architecture review cycles.

## Where the AI question lands

Their current focus is what AI-assisted development does to the security picture, and the answer is not to slow it down: speed without threat context is simply accelerated risk. The architecture sets the requirements, the coding agent inherits them as guardrails, and every output is validated before it ships.
