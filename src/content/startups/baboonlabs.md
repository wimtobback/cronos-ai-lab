Baboon Labs is a digital product lab headquartered in Belgium and part of The Cronos Group. It builds small, custom tools for the problems teams have learned to work around — the approval chain that runs on email, the onboarding nobody owns, the customer question that always becomes a ticket.

## How they work

A discovery assessment frames the problem, maps the user journey and sets go/no-go criteria. Design and build follows, with coding agents and engineers co-creating a working V1 in weeks rather than months. What works then gets hardened and connected to the stack that already exists — auth, data, ERP, CRM — with documentation and a support handover to the team that will run it.

## What has come out of it

Onboarding for HR, so people are ready on day one. One-click approvals for expenses, purchase orders and leave. Customer self-service for order and appointment status and simple returns. A proactive CRM assistant that captures visit notes by voice or text and pushes them straight through with next steps. A loyalty app, and a status dashboard that raises alerts on orders, incidents and SLAs early enough to act on.

## The bet

Everything is custom, and deliberately small: start from the problem and design the smallest tool that fixes it, not the other way around. Security, compliance and human approval sit inside the loop rather than after it.
