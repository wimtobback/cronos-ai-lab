AIGENEERS is two things at once: a name for a job that did not have one, and the platform where the people doing that job write about it.

## The role

An AIGENEER is a forward deployed engineer — someone who works inside the problem rather than alongside it, pairing human judgement with the speed and scale of AI systems. Their own framing is that AI should act as an exoskeleton for human expertise, amplifying it rather than replacing it: humans not merely in the loop, but in control. The title is already in use elsewhere in the network — Sliceworkz lists a Lead AIGeneer on its team.

## The platform

A living knowledge hub of articles and insights, each one signed by the practitioner who wrote it and filed under EuroStack, agentic AI, GenAI, infrastructure, hardware, strategy, governance or design. Everything is written by AIGENEERS in close collaboration with AI, then shared, challenged and improved by the community rather than published and left alone.

## European by construction

A Belgian company running its platform and tooling entirely on European infrastructure — OVH servers in France. The position is stated plainly on the site: Europe should not rent its intelligence from elsewhere, it should stand on its own EuroStack, and the platform starts by standing on it itself.
