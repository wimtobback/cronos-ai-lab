Most software projects start fast and then slow to a crawl. Complexity compounds, budgets grow with it, and the team ends up refactoring its way out of decisions nobody wrote down. Sliceworkz argues the cause is structural rather than technical, and rebuilds the practice around that.

## Four methods, as the foundation

Domain-driven design to ground the solution in business concepts. Event modelling to specify it visually, in business language, with the people who understand the domain. Vertical slice architecture so features stay isolated and can be built in parallel at constant cost. Event sourcing for history, audit and compliance. Not bolted on as practices — the structure everything else is built on.

## AI-native by design, not retrofit

Rather than adding AI to an existing process, the practice was designed around it: the methodologies, the project structure and the delivery model are all shaped for AI-assisted development. The claim they make for it is a cost curve that grows linearly instead of exponentially, and change driven by the business model rather than by accumulated accidental complexity.

## Where it comes from

A brand of XTi — Xtreme Integrations NV, drawing on 25+ years of enterprise delivery: a 40-plus team with roots in large-scale enterprise applications and complex integration work, used to structured SDLC and enterprise compliance. Offices in Kontich, Merelbeke and Hasselt. They maintain open source alongside the client work.
